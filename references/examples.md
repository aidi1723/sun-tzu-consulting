# Consulting Examples

Use these examples as adaptation patterns. Do not copy them mechanically; fit the Five Factors to the client's actual evidence and constraints.

## Traditional Education Institution Building an AI Tutoring App

User input:

> We are a traditional offline education institution. Offline enrollment is shrinking. We are considering spending 5M RMB to build an AI tutoring app. Is this feasible?

L1 diagnosis:

The client is trying to solve a channel and capability crisis by entering a red-ocean AI product battlefield where it lacks native product, data, and growth DNA.

Illustrative scores:

```json
{"dao":5,"tian":4,"di":3,"jiang":3,"fa":4}
```

Verdict:

否定原方案 or 重构方案. Do not directly build a consumer AI tutoring app with a new internal product team.

Reasoning:

- 道: Demand for better tutoring exists, but another generic app has weak differentiation.
- 天: AI education attention is high, but regulation, model cost, and CAC are uncertain.
- 地: Large platforms, AI-native startups, and content ecosystems already control attention and technology.
- 将: Offline education operators usually lack AI product, growth, and model iteration DNA.
- 法: Existing KPIs, teacher incentives, and offline operating cadence will resist fast product iteration.

Constructive alternative:

Use offline assets as the wedge: proprietary teaching data, local trust, teacher explanations, parent relationships, and paid tutoring scenarios.

L2 handoff preview:

- `借尸还魂`: acquire or partner with a distressed AI education team instead of building from scratch.
- `暗度陈仓`: maintain offline business as cash flow while using private teaching data to train a differentiated solution.
- `树上开花`: add credible AI researchers, education experts, or institutional partners before fundraising or strategic sale.

## Manufacturing Company Moving to SaaS

L1 diagnosis:

The initiative may be strategically attractive if it productizes internal workflow expertise, but it will fail if treated as a software side project under a manufacturing KPI system.

Watch:

- 道: Is there repeatable customer pain beyond the company's own factory?
- 天: Are target customers digitizing budgets now?
- 地: Are ERP/MES incumbents blocking distribution?
- 将: Is there a real SaaS GM with product-led growth experience?
- 法: Are recurring revenue, churn, support, and roadmap metrics separated from manufacturing KPIs?

Likely decision:

小规模试点 with strict kill criteria.

## Acquisition of a Competitor

L1 diagnosis:

The acquisition is only rational if it buys terrain or capability that cannot be built cheaper internally.

Watch:

- 道: Does the acquired customer base deepen the strategic identity?
- 天: Is the cycle favorable for buying distressed assets?
- 地: Does the target control channel, license, data, supply, or customer lock-in?
- 将: Can the integration team retain key people?
- 法: Can systems, incentives, finance, and culture integrate without destroying value?

Likely L2 needs:

If integration politics are the key risk, hand off to `thirty-six-strategies` for stakeholder sequencing, retention design, and negotiation strategy.
