# Sun Tzu Consulting Assessment Framework

Use this reference when producing a formal strategy audit, feasibility report, radar-chart data, risk score, or board-style consulting memo.

## Scoring Method

Score each factor from 0 to 10.

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0-2 | Fatal weakness; proceeding likely destroys capital or reputation. |
| 3-4 | High risk; initiative needs redesign before material investment. |
| 5-6 | Mixed; only proceed through controlled experiment. |
| 7-8 | Strong; proceed with risk controls and milestones. |
| 9-10 | Rare advantage; accelerate while defending the moat. |

Recommended decision bands:

| Average | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| 8.0+ | 推进 |
| 6.5-7.9 | 推进或试点, depending on downside and evidence quality |
| 5.0-6.4 | 小规模试点 |
| 3.5-4.9 | 重构方案 |
| <3.5 | 否定原方案 |

Override the average when any factor is fatal. For example, illegal regulatory exposure under `天`, nonexistent market demand under `道`, or an incapable leadership team under `将` can invalidate the strategy even when other scores look acceptable.

## Five Factors Translation

### 道 / Vision & Alignment

Consulting translation: product-market fit, business model coherence, user value, brand permission, and strategic identity.

Ask:
- Is this solving a real urgent customer problem?
- Is the revenue model connected to value created, not just traffic or subsidy?
- Does the client have a credible reason to win this market?
- Does the initiative reinforce or confuse the company's identity?

Evidence:
- Customer interviews, retention, paid pilots, win/loss data, NPS, cohort behavior, margin logic, brand perception.

Red flags:
- Strategy is driven by trend anxiety.
- Users like the concept but do not pay or switch behavior.
- Value proposition depends on subsidies, regulatory arbitrage, or vague "AI empowerment."

### 天 / Macro & Timing

Consulting translation: macro cycle, capital environment, policy, technology maturity, adoption readiness, and timing window.

Ask:
- Is regulation supportive, uncertain, or hostile?
- Are capital markets rewarding or punishing this kind of move?
- Is the enabling technology mature enough for the promise?
- Is the customer budget cycle open?

Evidence:
- Policy documents, funding patterns, adoption curves, procurement cycles, technology benchmarks, macro demand signals.

Red flags:
- Policy risk can shut down the model.
- Client enters after customer acquisition costs have already inflated.
- Technology is demo-ready but not production-ready.

### 地 / Market Terrain & Barriers

Consulting translation: market structure, channel control, competitive density, substitutes, switching costs, and moat.

Ask:
- Is the market red ocean, blue ocean, or a niche wedge?
- Who controls acquisition channels, supply, distribution, standards, or data?
- What barrier protects the client after launch?
- Is there a beachhead segment where the client has asymmetric advantage?

Evidence:
- Porter Five Forces, channel maps, margin pool analysis, competitor unit economics, procurement map, data/control point analysis.

Red flags:
- Competing head-on with better-funded incumbents.
- No clear wedge, moat, or channel access.
- Strategy depends on buying traffic at worsening economics.

### 将 / Team DNA & Leadership

Consulting translation: founder/management fit, capability model, decision speed, talent density, incentive alignment, and change leadership.

Ask:
- Has the team won in a structurally similar game before?
- Does leadership understand the new business model at operating level?
- Are key roles already in place?
- Will internal incentives support the new strategy or defend the old model?

Evidence:
- Team resumes, prior execution record, org chart, hiring funnel, leadership cadence, capability gap matrix.

Red flags:
- Traditional team attempts a digital-native strategy without native operators.
- Critical capability is outsourced but strategically central.
- Leadership wants transformation without changing power structure.

### 法 / Organization & SOP

Consulting translation: operating model, governance, KPI system, data process, budget discipline, compliance, and execution rhythm.

Ask:
- Does the operating model support the strategy?
- Are KPIs connected to the desired behavior?
- Are budget, ownership, and decision rights clear?
- Are compliance, data, HR, finance, and reporting processes ready?

Evidence:
- KPI tree, SOPs, budget model, governance cadence, compliance map, data architecture, incentive plan.

Red flags:
- Strategy is announced without owners, milestones, or kill criteria.
- KPI system rewards old behavior.
- Organization has no data loop for learning from pilots.

## Seven Calculations

Use these as a comparative layer between client vs competitor, current plan vs alternative, or acquisition target vs build option:

1. Which side has stronger strategic alignment and stakeholder support?
2. Which side has more favorable timing and external conditions?
3. Which side controls terrain, channel, customer access, data, or standards?
4. Which side has stronger leadership and talent fit?
5. Which side has clearer operating discipline and governance?
6. Which side has stronger resources, capital patience, and cost structure?
7. Which side can execute faster with fewer coordination losses?

## Report Quality Checklist

- Every score has at least one evidence line or explicit assumption.
- Fatal risks are called out even if average score is acceptable.
- The recommendation contains a budget posture: invest, cap, pause, or stop.
- The plan defines early signals and kill criteria.
- If the strategy is denied, the report gives a constructive alternative or L2 handoff.
